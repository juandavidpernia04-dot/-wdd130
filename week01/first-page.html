The Role of Input in Second Language Acquisition

Summary Paragraph:

📚 Second language acquisition is the process of learning a language other than one's first language. 🌍 One of the most important factors in this process is comprehensible input, which means learners are exposed to language they can mostly understand, even if some words are new. 🗣️ Research shows that people learn languages more effectively through meaningful listening and reading rather than memorizing grammar rules alone. ✨ As learners receive more understandable input, they gradually build vocabulary, improve grammar, and develop confidence in communication. 🚀 Over time, this helps them use the language naturally in real-life situations. 😊
